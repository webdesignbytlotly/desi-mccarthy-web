ABOUT ME

Hi I am Desi McCarthy, and I am a passionate natural light photographer based in the heart of Bloemfontein, South Africa. My photographic journey began many years ago with a simple desire: to freeze the fleeting moments of life that are often missed in the rush of the everyday. I specialize in creating timeless, emotive portraits, focusing on connection, real emotion, and the subtle interplay of light and shadow. Whether I am capturing the tender beginnings of a new family, the deep bonds of a couple, or the proud culmination of a graduation, my aim is to deliver authentic images that become cherished family heirlooms. Photography, for me, is about translating feelings into visuals, ensuring that your unique story is not just seen, but felt.
